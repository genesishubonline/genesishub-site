Services

Everything you need to enter Japan, from one partner

Japan is a large, high-value market with its own language, platforms and business customs. We give you the local expertise and the hands to act on it, scoped to your goals and budget, from a single market assessment to long-term execution support.

Market Analysis & Recommendations

Competitive landscape, user behavior, pricing, channels and regulatory considerations for your category in Japan, ending in clear recommendations on whether and how to enter.

Go-to-Market Strategy

Positioning, pricing, channel and partner strategy built around how Japanese customers actually evaluate and buy, plus a concrete launch plan with milestones.

Marketing & Advertising

Campaign planning and hands-on execution: paid media on Google, Meta, X, LINE and TikTok, social media operations, KOL and influencer programs, and events, all run in Japanese.

Localization & Translation

Websites, apps, games and business documents adapted for meaning and tone, not just words. Natural Japanese that reads as if it were written here, and English that works for your global team.

Business Matching & Partnerships

Introductions to the right Japanese partners for your goals, from startups to large corporations, with bilingual support through first meetings and negotiation.

Local Service Operation

We publish and operate consumer services for Japanese users, including LINE mini apps, and can act as the local service provider for your product in Japan.
